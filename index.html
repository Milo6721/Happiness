Essay about Happiness

Miłosz Górski and Anna Kowalska Petrie 2026

In my opinion, people should be happier, because happy a life is a great life. Happiness is something people search for in their lives. Nobody wants to be sad, right? Because sad people are unhappy people.

Scientists have found out that happier people live longer than sad and miserable ones. Nobody wants these misery guts around.

“Laugh and the whole world laughs with you, cry and you cry alone.” — old saying

Everyone knows that life is short and if you are happy, you will enjoy it. Many people search for happiness in their short lives and never find it. Happiness is not something you can buy, so don't look for it in the local shop.

Being in the company of happy people is much easier than being with sad people.

Happy people won't have so many illnesses as sad people. Medicine has proved it, 89% of people in hospitals are sad.

We all must stay calm and must not panic. You must stay happy and everything will be OK. If you stay happy and jolly, your life will be healthier, easier and longer.

While you're here — spread a little happiness.
